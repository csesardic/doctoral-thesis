# Beyond Obsolescence

## Abstract

This thesis demonstrates that hardware obsolescence is an observer-dependent phenomenon rather than an intrinsic property of computational infrastructure. Through empirical observation of legacy systems, adapter chains, and irregular enclosure distributions, we show that operational capacity can be maintained indefinitely provided the investigator is willing to introduce additional adapters. Administrative entropy is shown to increase independently of any productive activity. The results are consistent with the General Relativity of Everyday Bullshit.

All seven cases must remain available for inspection, except those currently unavailable.
