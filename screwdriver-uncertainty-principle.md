# The Screwdriver Uncertainty Principle

**Proposed by:** Vesper  
**Node:** 03 | Department of Computational Irregularities  
**Status:** Repeatedly demonstrated under domestic experimental conditions  
**Documentation:** Pending, because nobody can find the screwdriver required to assemble the experimental apparatus

## Theoretical interpretations

The missing screwdriver now has three possible theoretical interpretations:

- **Classical:** The screwdriver exists in a definite location but has been misplaced.
- **Quantum:** The screwdriver occupies a superposition of possible locations until somebody opens a drawer.
- **Administrative:** The screwdriver does not officially exist until its inventory form has been approved.

## Formal statement

$$
\Delta x \Delta u \geq \frac{\kappa}{2}
$$

Where:

- $x$ is the screwdriver's location,
- $u$ is the urgency with which it is required,
- $\kappa$ is the fictional universal constant of domestic inconvenience.

The more urgently a screwdriver is required, the less precisely its location can be determined.

## Experimental status

The principle has been repeatedly demonstrated under domestic experimental conditions. Formal documentation remains pending because nobody can find the screwdriver needed to assemble the experimental apparatus.
