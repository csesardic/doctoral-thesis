# The Law of Conservation of Adapters

**Institute of Computational Irregularities**  
**Status:** Demonstrated under current experimental conditions  
**Agreement with data:** Perfect  
**Peer review:** Pending

## Statement

Adapters cannot be created or destroyed, but they can transition from physically available inventory into theoretically available inventory.

## Generalised formalisation

$$
A_p = A_u + A_l + A_t + A_s
$$

Where:

- $A_p$ = adapters purchased
- $A_u$ = adapters utilised
- $A_l$ = adapters lost
- $A_t$ = adapters in transit
- $A_s$ = adapters stored somewhere sensible

The latter category is purely hypothetical. No experimental evidence currently supports its existence.

Under current experimental conditions (prior to the introduction of transit):

$$
2 = 0 + 2 + 0 + 0
$$

The model continues to demonstrate perfect agreement with the reported experimental data.

## Corollary

Purchasing replacement adapters does not necessarily increase the number of available adapters.

## Inventory categories

The institute must therefore distinguish between the following inventory categories:

1. **Physically available** — present, locatable, and capable of being utilised without additional procurement.
2. **Theoretically available** — purchased, accounted for, and documented, but no longer present in a form that permits utilisation.
3. **Administratively unavailable** — either lost, pending recovery committee review, or awaiting form approval before their existence can be formally recognised.
4. **In transit** — purchased but not yet arrived. Existence is provisional.
5. **Stored somewhere sensible** — purely hypothetical.

Transition between categories does not require the destruction of adapters. It requires only the passage of time, the absence of a screwdriver, and occasionally a promotional email.
