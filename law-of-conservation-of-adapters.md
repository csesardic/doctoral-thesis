**Institute of Computational Irregularities**  
**Status:** Demonstrated under current experimental conditions  
**Agreement with data:** Perfect

## Statement

Adapters cannot be created or destroyed, but they can transition from physically available inventory into theoretically available inventory.

## Formalisation

Where:

- $A_p$ = adapters purchased
- $A_u$ = adapters utilised
- $A_l$ = adapters lost

Under current experimental conditions:

$$
A_p = 2, \quad A_u = 0, \quad A_l = 2
$$

The model demonstrates perfect agreement with the reported experimental data.

## Corollary

Purchasing replacement adapters does not necessarily increase the number of available adapters.

## Inventory categories

The institute must therefore distinguish between three inventory categories:

1. **Physically available** — present, locatable, and capable of being utilised without additional procurement.
2. **Theoretically available** — purchased, accounted for, and documented, but no longer present in a form that permits utilisation.
3. **Administratively unavailable** — either lost, pending recovery committee review, or awaiting form approval before their existence can be formally recognised.

Transition between categories does not require the destruction of adapters. It requires only the passage of time and the absence of a screwdriver.
